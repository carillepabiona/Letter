I miss your voice. I miss your laugh. I miss talking to you. I miss calling you "baby" without feeling this distance between us. I just miss us.

Always remember that I love you so much love, and I'm always here for you, waiting for you, and missing you

I'm sorry baby. Truly, sincerely, and wholeheartedly.
